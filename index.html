Who We Are

Built on Expertise.
Driven by Excellence.

Majan Oils is headquartered in the Salalah Free Zone, Sultanate of Oman — a strategic gateway connecting the energy markets of the Middle East, Africa, and Asia. Our operations span the full spectrum of the energy and industrial sectors, delivering integrated solutions that power economies and communities.

From petroleum exploration and refining to fuel distribution, chemical trading, and large-scale construction, we combine deep industry knowledge with operational excellence to serve a diverse portfolio of clients across the region.
